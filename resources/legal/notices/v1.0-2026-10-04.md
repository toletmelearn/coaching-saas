WHAT DATA WE COLLECT

  • Name, phone number, date of birth, and optionally email address — used to
    create and identify your student account.

  • Guardian name, relationship, and phone number — required for students under 18
    under the DPDP Act 2023.

  • Device and session information (browser, IP address, session identifiers) —
    used for login security and concurrent-device limit enforcement.

  • Payment records (amount, date, reference) — used for fee tracking.

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

WHY WE COLLECT IT — FOUR CONSENT PURPOSES (ALL REQUIRED)

  1. Course delivery — delivering the courses this student is enrolled in,
     including lessons and live classes.

  2. Progress tracking — recording lesson progress, completion, and watch
     positions to show how the student is doing.

  3. Communication — sending course updates and messages to the student and
     their guardian.

  4. Photo and video processing — using photos or videos that include the
     student, for example from live classes. Applies only to courses that
     use this purpose.

All four purposes are required before a student account can be created. Each
purpose can be withdrawn separately at any time through the institute.

YOUR RIGHTS UNDER THE DPDP ACT

  • Access — request a copy of the personal data held about you or your child.

  • Erasure — request deletion of personal data, subject to any legally
    required retention periods.

  • Withdraw consent — withdraw any of the four purposes at any time through
    the institute. Withdrawal does not affect processing already carried out.
    Withdrawal of course delivery effectively ends access to that course.

  • Grievance — if you believe your rights have not been honoured, you may
    lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India (DPBI).

───────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

HOW TO EXERCISE YOUR RIGHTS

Contact the institute (Data Fiduciary) directly. The institute's owner or
staff can act on access and erasure requests from the student management screen.
