WHAT DATA WE COLLECT AND WHY

We collect personal data only for the purposes listed below. All four consent
purposes must be agreed to before a student account can be created.

1. Course delivery
   Data: name, phone number, date of birth, email (optional), IP address, browser
   information, session identifiers, payment amount, payment date, UPI reference,
   live class attendance flag, join time, watch duration.
   We use this data to give the student access to enrolled courses, lessons, and
   live classes, and to process fee payments.

2. Progress tracking
   Data: lesson watch positions, lesson completion flags, last-accessed timestamps,
   live class watch duration.
   We use this data to show the student and the institute how the student is
   progressing through their enrolled courses.

3. Communication
   Data: name, phone number, email (optional), guardian name, guardian phone number,
   guardian email (optional), guardian relationship to student.
   We use this data to send course updates, payment reminders, and messages to the
   student and their guardian.

4. Photo and video processing
   Data: recordings or images from live classes that include the student.
   We use this data to process class recordings where applicable. This purpose
   applies only to courses that use live-class recordings.

For students under 18, guardian details (name, relationship, phone, optional email)
are also collected and are required before account creation.

ADDITIONAL DATA COLLECTED FOR PLATFORM OPERATION

Device and session data (IP address, browser type, session identifiers, login
timestamps) are also processed under legitimate interest to enforce device limits,
detect unauthorised access, and maintain the security of the platform. This
processing is not conditional on the four consent purposes above.

GUARDIAN CONSENT FOR STUDENTS UNDER 18

The DPDP Act 2023 requires verifiable parental or guardian consent before
processing the personal data of anyone under 18. A student account cannot be
created until the institute records that a guardian has agreed to all four purposes.

Each purpose can be withdrawn separately at any time through the institute.
Withdrawing course delivery ends the student's access to that course.

YOUR RIGHTS UNDER THE DPDP ACT 2023

Right to access — You may request a copy of the personal data the institute holds
about you or your child.

Right to correction — You may request that inaccurate or incomplete personal data
be corrected.

Right to erasure — You may request deletion of personal data, subject to any
retention periods required by law or contract.

Right to withdraw consent — You may withdraw consent for any of the four purposes
at any time through the institute. Withdrawal does not affect processing already
carried out lawfully before the withdrawal.

Right to grievance redressal — If you believe your rights have not been honoured,
you may first raise a grievance with the institute's Grievance Officer (contact
above). If unresolved, you may make a formal complaint to the Data Protection Board
of India (DPBI). To make a complaint to the DPBI, follow the formal complaint
procedure. [TO BE CONFIRMED BY OPERATOR: DPBI complaint portal URL, exact procedure,
and any applicable timelines under DPDP Rules 2025]
